Questions before you order

What is the difference between normal and toughened glass?

Toughened glass is heated and cooled in a controlled way so it becomes several times stronger. If it breaks, it crumbles into small, blunt pieces instead of long, sharp ones. Try the strength test above.

Can toughened glass be cut after it is made?

No. Cutting, drilling and polishing must be done before toughening. That is why we measure on site and confirm the size with you first.

Which glass should I use for a balcony railing?

Toughened glass of 12 mm or more, or laminated toughened glass. Laminated glass stays held together by its film if one sheet cracks.

Will double glazing really help with heat and noise?

Yes. The sealed air gap between the two panes slows down heat and sound. It helps most on sun-facing or road-facing windows in air-conditioned rooms.

Do you supply hinges, handles and other fittings?

Yes. Fittings are chosen to match the weight and thickness of the glass, so doors and showers open smoothly and last.

Is the site visit really free?

Yes. We visit, measure, suggest the right glass and give you a written quote with no obligation.
